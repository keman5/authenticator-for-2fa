## Full description — Chrome, Edge, Firefox

Generate and manage two-factor verification codes in your browser with Authenticator-For-2FA.

• TOTP, HOTP and Steam codes, generated on your device.
• Add accounts manually or import OTP links, QR images, migration QR codes and supported backups.
• Scan QR codes from a selected page area, screen or camera after granting the required access.
• Search, favorite and reorder accounts. Click a code to copy it.
• Optionally fill a selected verification-code field without submitting the form. Website access is requested for optional input menus.
• Export password-protected backups, or explicitly choose plaintext export.
• Optionally sync encrypted account copies through your browser's sync service using a separate recovery key. The same browser sync account is required on your devices.
• Optionally lock the interface with a password. Passkeys depend on browser and system support; Touch ID on supported Macs requires a configured local companion.

Important: the screen lock does not encrypt local account secrets or the local sync recovery key. Password protection is off initially. Protect your browser profile and keep a verified backup and your services' recovery methods.

QR decoding runs locally using bundled code. There are no ads or usage analytics. Optional time calibration contacts Google after website permission; optional encrypted sync uses your browser vendor's service. Filling sends the selected code to the destination website. See the privacy policy for data handling and deletion, including retained account-deletion records.

Support and privacy questions: support@macseek.cc
